The active instructions are [Public kit quick start](PUBLIC-QUICK-START.md) and [Update the existing kit](UPDATE-EXISTING-KIT.md). No current kit size, SHA-256 or source revision is asserted in this guide; root must supply actual verified delivery pins first.

1. **Receive the next-step notice before using the Mac or its connection.** It must name the task, resources and selected Wi-Fi/hotspot connection, and state the exact verified transfer size or that usage is unknown. Routine approval already granted remains valid. Apple credential/certificate changes and a large Xcode installation retain their separate approval boundary.
2. **Download only the verified small native kit once.** Check its actual size/hash first. Do not download/clone the full repository or install/build Rust on the Mac; the kit carries the cloud-built library and USB helper.
3. **Use update-public-kit.py for the source-bound chooser/overlay into AI-Workspace/dokkan-offline-public.** Its default only describes the plan; the assistant supplies the actual reviewed size/hash/source and verified updater source before `--execute`. `--choose` selects local paths. Preserve that folder's unlisted ignored private files and the installed app/data. Never open or modify the frozen old backup. Do not substitute historical paths, downloads or hashes.
4. **Update the app only if the installed build is incompatible with this kit.** Choose the same physical phone, existing development team and existing bundle identity. Keep the plan visible before running setup. Do not uninstall or reset trust/data. Provisioning may use an unknown amount of Apple internet traffic; announce it beforehand.
5. **For a pairing retry, choose pairing-only.** It must not build, sign, reinstall or relaunch the app, discover Apple accounts or change credentials. Make the strict pinned version-2 private export through the verified USB helper. Transfer/import it directly to the same phone; keep its contents and device/account identifiers out of chat, email and GitHub.
6. **Check the phone connection.** Enter any needed numeric local/VPN address in App Installer Settings locally, keep LocalDevVPN connected, then unlock and check. “Saved” is not “Connection verified.” Report only fixed safe status codes if it fails.
7. **Proceed to the next actual acceptance check.** Same-app update, refresh, restart/protected storage, backup recovery and physical Duo behavior are not established by a build or successful pairing export.

Passwords/codes stay in Apple's local UI or on the phone. No raw private logs or records are needed. Stop before any newly reserved account/certificate or major Xcode change, rather than repeating an already-approved routine-permission question.
